Daily Spark is a calm, distraction-free, 100% offline-first habit tracker.
Track one small action a day per habit. No account, no analytics, no ads, no social feeds, and no pushy guilt-inducing streaks.

Key Features:
* 100% Offline-First: All habit records stay on your physical device.
* Home-Screen Android Widgets: Glanceable Today checklist, Streak counter, and Heatmap.
* Activity Matrix: 6-month habit activity heatmap.
* Zero Forced Permissions: Core tracking works without internet or special privileges.
* Open Data: Instant encrypted JSON backup export and import anytime.
